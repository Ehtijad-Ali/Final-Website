Our Story

Our Journey

Building the future of independent music, one artist at a time.

At HABIBI Music Agency, we are a creative team dedicated to helping independent artists build professional music careers. We started with one goal — to make high-quality music services accessible to every artist, no matter where they are in the world. From professional music mastering and cover art design to artist branding, promotion, Google Knowledge Panels, and sync licensing, our team provides everything artists need to grow their presence. Today, we work with artists worldwide, helping them sound better, look professional, and reach bigger audiences across digital platforms.

Our Mission

To empower independent artists with professional tools, creative solutions, and strategies that help them succeed.

Our Vision

Our Goal

To help every artist turn their music into a powerful brand.
