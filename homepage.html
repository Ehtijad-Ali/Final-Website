FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before starting your project.

1. How fast do you deliver?

Standard delivery is 48 hours for single-track mastering. Complex projects (albums, stems, or video production) typically take 3–7 business days. Rush delivery options are available for an additional fee; contact us for exact timing.

2. Do you offer revisions?

Yes — we include one free revision for mastering and two rounds for visual assets. Additional revisions can be requested and are charged only when they exceed the agreed scope.

3. What file formats do I need to send?

For mastering, send high-resolution WAV or AIFF (24-bit, 44.1/48/96kHz). For stems, provide labeled WAV files. For artwork, supply 3000×3000px PNG or JPG at 300 DPI. We accept large file transfers via WeTransfer, Google Drive, or Dropbox.

4. What payment methods do you accept?

We accept major credit/debit cards via Stripe, PayPal, and bank transfers for larger invoices. Payment is required before final delivery unless other arrangements are agreed in writing.

5. Do you work with artists worldwide?

Absolutely — we work with artists globally. Most collaboration is handled remotely through file transfers and video calls. Timezone-friendly scheduling is available for consultations.
